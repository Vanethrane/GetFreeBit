Bitcoin calculators

Pick the tool that matches your question. Each page owns one search intent—no thin clones.

Live USD legs use public spot feeds. Network tools use transparent assumptions—not investment advice. Each tool page lists Source (primary docs) and GetFreeBit analysis (calculator assumptions) separately.

Converters

  • bitcoin calculator

    Bitcoin calculator

    One amount field. BTC or sats in; USD and the other unit out. Built for operators who leave a tab open.

  • satoshi calculator

    Satoshi calculator

    Start in sats. See BTC and dollars without rewriting scientific notation.

  • btc to usd

    BTC to USD

    Directional converter: Bitcoin in, dollars out—plus sats as a secondary read.

  • usd to btc

    USD to BTC

    Budget in dollars, see BTC and sats before you hit buy.

  • btc to sats

    BTC to sats

    Pure unit conversion. Price is irrelevant; the scale is protocol-fixed.

  • sats to btc

    Sats to BTC

    Integer sats in, BTC with up to eight decimals out.

  • sats to usd

    Sats to USD

    Micro amounts in, dollar snapshot out—plus BTC as a secondary line.

  • usd to sats

    USD to sats

    Dollar budget in; sats (and BTC) out at today’s mark.

Planning & PnL

  • bitcoin fee calculator

    Bitcoin fee calculator

    Fee = rate × virtual size. Pick a template, set sats/vB, see the bill.

  • bitcoin dca calculator

    Bitcoin DCA calculator

    Simple projection: same USD each period at a flat price assumption. Not backtesting.

  • bitcoin profit calculator

    Bitcoin profit calculator

    Mark-to-market PnL: holdings × spot − cost basis.

Network & supply

  • bitcoin halving countdown

    Bitcoin halving countdown

    Track the next subsidy cut. Understand issuance, not hopium.

  • bitcoin mining calculator

    Bitcoin mining calculator

    Gross share of subsidy, minus power and pool fees. Hardware cost and difficulty change are on you.

  • bitcoin block reward

    Bitcoin block reward calculator

    Subsidy table + live USD mark on today’s block reward.

  • bitcoin inflation rate

    Bitcoin inflation calculator

    Issuance ÷ circulating supply. A supply metric—not CPI and not a price forecast.
