The fixed ratio

Bitcoin’s consensus treats 1 BTC as 100,000,000 base units (sats). Converting BTC → sats is multiply by 1e8 and round to a whole sat for display.

When you need this

Wallets and explorers often show BTC with eight decimals. Faucets and Lightning UX prefer integer sats. Convert before you copy an amount into the wrong field.
