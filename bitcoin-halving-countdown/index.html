What a halving does

Every 210,000 blocks (~4 years), the block subsidy halves. Fees still exist; new issuance drops. That is a supply schedule event—not a guaranteed price event.

Why the date is an estimate

Blocks average ~10 minutes but vary. Countdown clocks use an estimated timestamp and target height. Actual wall-clock time drifts with hashrate and luck.

After the cut

Miners earn fewer new BTC per block. Security budget shifts toward fees over decades. Pair this page with the block-reward and inflation tools for the numbers.

FAQ

When is the next Bitcoin halving?
The next subsidy cut is targeted near block 1,050,000, often estimated around April 2028. Exact UTC time depends on block intervals.
Does a Bitcoin halving guarantee a higher price?
No. Issuance falls; demand is independent. Historical price paths are not promises.
What is the current block subsidy?
After the 2024 halving, the subsidy is 3.125 BTC per block until the next halving.
