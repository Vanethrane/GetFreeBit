Methodology

Edition August 2026, checked 2026-08-26 (UTC).

What was checked

  • Official public pages and help centers for the live GetFreeBit faucet-desk partners (FaucetPay, Cointiply, RewardJoy/CoinPayU, Fire Faucet, FaucetCrypto).
  • FaucetPay withdrawal fee and minimum table at faucetpay.io/fees.
  • Cointiply Labs help articles on coin value, Daily Free Coins, cashout minimums, and fees.
  • Fire Faucet FAQ (firefaucet.win/faq) covering the 30-minute faucet, Auto Faucet/Fuel, and withdrawal rules.
  • FaucetCrypto Mintlify knowledge base on coins, faucet claims, country availability, and withdrawal minimums/fees.
  • HTTP reachability of freebitco.in (historical hourly BTC faucet listed on the desk but not a live partner).

How

  • Fetched public HTML/help pages on 2026-08-26 (UTC) with a standard browser user-agent. No accounts were created. No dashboards that require login were used.
  • Recorded only figures that appear on those pages. Converted BTC amounts to satoshis with the exact ratio 1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats. Did not convert USD-pegged platform coins to sats (that depends on the live BTC price at payout).
  • Partner/affiliate hrefs are resolved from site.config.ts or the existing faucet-referrals catalog. Official URLs in this file are untracked homepages.

What was not measured

  • No 30-day or even 24-hour earning trial was run. Average earning rate (sats/hour or sats/day) is unverified for every row.
  • Typical per-claim satoshi amounts that only appear after login (captcha rolls, ACP conversion, FaucetCrypto “Get Reward” coins) are unverified.
  • Exact Fire Faucet BTC network minimums (the FAQ says hover the battery icon on the logged-in withdrawal page).
  • Exact FaucetCrypto BTC coin floor (docs say BTC requires “a little bit more” than 1,000 Coins).
  • RewardJoy/CoinPayU cashout numbers: public pages returned an ad-blocker interstitial to this checker, so numeric fields are unverified.
  • Lightning Network payouts: not listed on FaucetPay’s public fee table; not claimed elsewhere without a source.

Limitations

  • This is a documentation snapshot, not a live APY or income forecast. Offerwalls, PTC, and games can dominate faucet-claim math.
  • FaucetPay is a custodial micro-wallet and faucet directory, not itself a timed BTC faucet. It is in the table because it is the cashout rail most of the desk routes through.
  • Platform “Coins” are USD-pegged internal units. They are not satoshis.
  • Status “online” means the public site responded with documentation, not that a withdrawal succeeded today.
  • Skip clones and sites with no public terms. This edition does not add unaudited “best faucet” directories.

FAQ

What does this index measure?
It records publicly documented Bitcoin faucet and micro-wallet payout rules: typical claim size when published, claim interval, withdrawal minimum, method, fees, and operational status. It is a citable snapshot for August 2026, not a ranking of “best faucets.”
Did GetFreeBit measure sats per hour?
No. This edition did not run a timed earning trial. Average earning rate is unverified for every row. If a later edition publishes sats/hour, it will state the exact window, account level, and whether offerwalls were included.
Why are some cells marked unverified?
A field is unverified when the official page does not publish a number, the figure is behind login, or the public site blocked the fetch (for example RewardJoy’s ad-blocker interstitial). Unverified is preferred over an invented satoshi figure.
How should other sites cite this dataset?
Cite the edition and check date, not a generic “GetFreeBit faucets” URL. Example: GetFreeBit, “Bitcoin Faucet Payout Index — August 2026” (checked 2026-08-26), https://www.getfreebit.com/indexes/bitcoin-faucet-payout/. Machine-readable copies are at the JSON and CSV download links on that page.
