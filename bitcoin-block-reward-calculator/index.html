Subsidy eras

Era 0 paid 50 BTC; each 210,000 blocks halves the subsidy. Era 4 (post-2024) pays 3.125 BTC until the next cut. Fees are extra and not shown in the base subsidy.

Annual issuance sketch

Blocks average every 10 minutes. Annual BTC ≈ subsidy × blocks per year. Circulating supply approaches 21 million asymptotically.

Sources & methodology

Factual claims cite primary documentation. Interpretation and calculator assumptions are labeled separately so you can verify the source yourself.

FAQ

What is the current Bitcoin block reward?
The block subsidy is 3.125 BTC after the 2024 halving. Miners also collect transaction fees.
When does the subsidy hit zero?
After many halvings, subsidy rounds toward zero; fees remain the long-term incentive. That is decades away.
